# Overlord

![[assets/Overlord.jpg]]

*Huge monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Orders pass from the Queen of Blades to broodmothers, from broodmothers to overlords, and from overlords to individual zerg. Overlords are the chain of communication. Their highly sensitive sensory organs can see through illusions and disguises, allowing them to gather battlefield intelligence and pass it down the chain.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 14\
**Hit Points** 120 (16d12 + 16)\
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) | 13 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 6 (-2) |

**Senses** truesight 90 ft., passive Perception 15\
**Languages** telepathy 90 ft.\
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)

## Traits

**Telepathic Connection.** Communicates what it senses to all zerg within 90 ft.

**Ventral Sacs.** Transports up to 16 space units (Tiny=1, Small=2, Medium=4, Large=8). Creatures inside die if the overlord dies.

## Actions

**Excrete Creep.** If unmoved since start of last turn: excretes creep in 20-ft. radius below. Zerg touching creep gain +10 ft. speed and regenerate 2 HP/turn. Creep dies at start of overlord's next turn.

**Load.** Loads one willing Large or smaller creature within 15 ft. (if space remains). Loaded creature has total cover and is incapacitated.

**Unload.** Unloads any number of creatures within 5 ft. Creates a fluid sphere allowing safe falls up to 100 ft.
