# Drone

![[assets/Drone.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Drones are the lowest members of the zerg hierarchy, nearly mindless creatures commonly seen collecting food or wood with lobster-like claws. Despite this, they are dearly protected because their bodies are the building blocks of zerg structures. Hundreds or thousands of drones can fuse together to create the swarm's living, breathing buildings.
