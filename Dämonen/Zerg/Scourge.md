# Scourge

![[assets/Scourge.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Scourge are often likened to flying banelings. Instead of acid, their suicide attack triggers an intense exothermic reaction, producing a blinding flash of heat and light and leaving poisonous gas behind. Their speed and sheer numbers make them effective anti-air and anti-naval weapons.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 14\
**Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)\
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 19 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 3 (-4) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |

**Damage Immunities** fire\
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

## Traits

**Death Throes.** On death: each creature within 30 ft. makes DC 15 Dex save — 35 (10d6) fire on fail, half on success. Ignites unattended flammables. Poisonous fumes for 1 minute (or dispersed by moderate wind): 11 (2d10) poison damage on enter/start of turn + can't breathe until leaving.

## Actions

**Suicide.** The scourge dies.
