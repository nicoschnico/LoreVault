# Guardian

![[assets/Guardian.jpg]]

*Huge monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Guardians are living turrets capable of launching balls of acid from more than a thousand feet away. They guard entrances to hives and large hatcheries, annihilating targets before they can approach the zerg structure or its larva. Their protective role also follows the swarm's expansion, where they act as living siege engines.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 14\
**Hit Points** 142 (15d12 + 45)\
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 20 ft. (hover)

**Senses** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 15\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

## Traits

**Far Sighted Artillery.** Disadvantage on attacks against creatures within 40 ft.

**Low Flying.** Cannot hover more than 40 ft. in the air.

**Siege Monster.** Deals double damage to objects and structures.

## Actions

**Acid Spores.** *Ranged:* +6 to hit, 300/1200 ft. *Hit:* 35 (5d12+3) acid damage.
