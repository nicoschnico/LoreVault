# Baneling

![[assets/Baneling.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Banelings are zerglings mutated beyond recognition. Their eyes have folded over and much of their body protrudes sacks of pulsating green acid potent enough to dissolve flesh and bone. They are used as living landmines and bombs, well suited to triggering catastrophic internal reactions that kill the baneling and release its payload.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 10\
**Hit Points** 27 (6d8)\
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +2\
**Damage Immunities** acid\
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 14\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

## Traits

**Burrow.** 1 minute to burrow up to 5 ft. below solid rock; total cover while submerged; unburrow as bonus action. 10+ damage while burrowed: Con save DC (5 + damage) or forced to unburrow.

**Death Throes.** On death, explodes. Each creature within 15 ft.: DC 13 Dex save or 21 (6d6) acid damage (half on success). Leaves a 5-ft.-radius acid pool (difficult terrain, 7 (2d6) acid on turn end) for 1 round.

## Actions

**Suicide.** The baneling dies.
