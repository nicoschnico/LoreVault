# Ultralisk

![[assets/Ultralisk.jpg]]

*Gargantuan monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Second only to the Queen of Blades, the ultralisk is the strongest creature in the zerg army. Its enormous kaiser blades and strength can rip through steel, while its steps leave footprints deep enough to resemble unfilled graves. Its gigantic armored plates are exceptionally resilient, and its simple mind makes it resistant to psychic horrors and charming effects.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 19 (natural armor)\
**Hit Points** 407 (22d20 + 176)\
**Speed** 40 ft.

**Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +15, Wis +9\
**Skills** Perception +9\
**Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical weapons\
**Damage Immunities** poison, psychic\
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned\
**Senses** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 19\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 21 (33,000 XP)

## Traits

**Cleave.** When attacking a creature, also rolls to attack all creatures within 5 ft. of the target.

**Siege Monster.** Deals double damage to objects and structures.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two Kaiser Blades attacks. Can replace either with Gore or Crush.

**Gore.** *Melee:* +15 to hit, 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (2d12+8) piercing. Large or smaller: DC 20 Str save or knocked prone.

**Kaiser Blades.** *Melee:* +15 to hit, 10 ft. *Hit:* 27 (3d12+8) slashing.

**Stomp.** *Melee:* +15 to hit, 10 ft., one prone creature. *Hit:* 40 (5d12+8) bludgeoning. DC 20 Str save or restrained. Repeat at start of each turn. While restrained, ultralisk is grappled. No Cleave.

## Legendary Actions (3/round)

- **Attack.** One Gore or Kaiser Blades attack.
- **Crush (2 actions).** Stomp on a restrained creature.
