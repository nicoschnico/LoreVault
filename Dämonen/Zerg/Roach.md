# Roach

![[assets/Roach.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Roaches descend from a hardy slug-like species capable of regenerating from wounds that would kill most creatures. Their current form features thick armored plates, tunneling claws, and corrosive saliva modeled after the mucus they once secreted. Their survivability makes them essential and extremely versatile members of the zerg army.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 13\
**Hit Points** 104 (11d8 + 55)\
**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 3 (-4) | 13 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5\
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 13\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

## Traits

**Adaptive Plating.** When below half HP, AC becomes 16 for 1 minute. Recharges after long rest.

**Regeneration.** Regains 5 HP at start of its turn if at 1+ HP and hasn't been damaged since end of its last turn.

## Actions

**Acidic Saliva.** *Ranged:* +5 to hit, 30/90 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d10+3) acid.
