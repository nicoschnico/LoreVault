# Mutalisk

![[assets/Mutalisk.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Mutalisks are the hit-and-run specialists of the zerg army: extremely nimble, fast, and able to dart in and out of combat. They are often seen destroying supply caravans or slaughtering farmers before disappearing. Their bouncing glaive worms make them useful for scattering enemies and making prey easier for the swarm to surround.

## Traits

**Regeneration.** Regains 5 HP at start of its turn if it has at least 1 HP and hasn't been damaged since the end of its last turn.

## Actions

**Glaive Wurm.** *Ranged:* +6 to hit, 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8+4) slashing. Bounces to another creature within 10 ft., then to a third within 10 ft. of the second. Separate attack roll for each.
