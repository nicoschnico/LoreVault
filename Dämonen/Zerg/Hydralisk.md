# Hydralisk

![[assets/Hydralisk.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Hydralisks are the backbone of the zerg army. Descended from a caterpillar-like race of herbivores, they have become clawed and armored serpents. Their needle spines deliver a debilitating poison used to slow and cripple attackers.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 13\
**Hit Points** 91 (14d8 + 28)\
**Speed** 35 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 3 (-4) | 15 (+2) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +6\
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 14\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

## Traits

**Ambusher.** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.

**Burrow.** Standard 1-minute zerg burrow.

**Poison Strikes.** Creature hit by Needle Spines or Claws: DC 13 Con save or poisoned for 1 minute (repeat at end of each turn). After success or end: immune to this hydralisk's Poison Strikes for 24 hours.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two Needle Spines attacks.

**Claw.** *Melee:* +4 to hit, 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6+3) slashing.

**Needle Spines.** *Ranged:* +7 to hit, 100/400 ft. *Hit:* 11 (1d12+5) piercing.
