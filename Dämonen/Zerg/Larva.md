# Larva

![[assets/Larva.jpg]]

*Tiny monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

A larva's maggot-like body consists of heavily armored segments, beneath which lies the complexity and potential of every strain in the swarm. The essence of every member of every strain is stored within it. Larva are therefore precious resources and are closely guarded. If a hive runs low, remaining larva may be released on livestock or townsfolk to infest and multiply.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 18\
**Hit Points** 14 (4d4 + 4)\
**Speed** 10 ft.

**Condition Immunities** poisoned\
**Senses** tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 14\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

## Traits

**Burrow.** Standard 1-minute zerg burrow.

**Morph.** After 1 day inside a shell, transforms into an [[Egg]].

## Actions

**Infest (1/Day).** One beast or humanoid within 5 ft.: DC 13 Con save or infested (larva burrows inside). At day 30: 1d8 piercing damage (unreducible) at start of each day, +2d8/day beyond 30th. Ends when creature drops to 0 HP → larva appears adjacent.
