# Defiler

![[assets/Defiler.jpg]]

*Large monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

The defiler corrupts, kills, and absorbs all it touches. Its body produces cancerous chemicals as a defense mechanism, and those chemicals can be used against other zerg, killing them and channeling the released energy into powerful spells. Defilers are rarely seen outside large hatcheries and hives, but where the swarm can support them, their destructive expenditure is rewarded by the havoc they cause.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 17\
**Hit Points** 135 (18d10 + 36)\
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 11 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 5 (-3) | 19 (+4) | 6 (-2) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Con +5, Wis +8, Cha +2\
**Skills** Perception +8\
**Damage Immunities** poison\
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned\
**Senses** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 18\
**Languages** telepathy 60 ft.\
**Challenge** 6 (1,800 XP)

## Traits

**Burrow.** 1 minute to burrow 5 ft. below solid rock; total cover; unburrow as bonus action. 10+ damage: Con save DC (5 + damage) or forced up.

**Innate Spellcasting.** Wis (save DC 15), no material components:
- At will: *blindness/deafness, enlarge/reduce, faerie fire, fog cloud, thunderwave*
- 3/day: *cloudkill, confusion, counterspell, crown of madness, Evard's black tentacles, fireball, hold person, hypnotic pattern, slow*
- 1/day: *cone of cold, dominate person, eyebite, insect plague*

## Actions

**Consume.** Instantly kills one willing living creature. Defiler regains 3 uses of a 3/day spell or 1 use of a 1/day spell. Cannot target charmed creatures.

**Dark Swarm (Recharge 5–6).** 15-ft.-radius opaque orange cloud at a point within 60 ft. for 1 minute. Creatures inside have resistance to ranged attacks.
