# Corruptor

![[assets/Corruptor.jpg]]

*Large monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

A central, brain-like mass dominates the corruptor's alien features, drawing attention away from the tentacles drifting around it and the beak-like protrusion that serves as a mouth. The size and power of its brain give it significant telepathic abilities and the power to dominate creatures it finds. Gifted with scheming intelligence by the Queen of Blades, corruptors disrupt battles, gather information, and infiltrate the swarm's enemies.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 14\
**Hit Points** 120 (16d10 + 32)\
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 6 (-2) |

**Senses** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 15\
**Languages** telepathy 60 ft.\
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

**Telepathic Bond.** Ignores range restriction on telepathy when communicating with a charmed creature. They don't need to be on the same plane.

## Actions

**Caustic Spray.** Target within 60 ft. (or itself, affecting creatures within 5 ft.): Dex save; fail → 14 (4d6) acid damage + 1d8 acid whenever it takes damage for 1 minute. Success → half, no ongoing.

**Parasite Spore.** *Ranged:* +5 to hit, 100/400 ft. *Hit:* 10 (6d8+3) piercing.

**Corruption.** One humanoid within 60 ft.: DC 14 Wis save or charmed for 1 day, obeying telepathic commands. Harm or suicidal command → repeat save. Success or end of effect → immune for 24 hours. One target at a time.
