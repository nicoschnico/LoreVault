# Swarm Host

![[assets/Swarm_Host.jpg]]

*Large monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

The swarm host is nearly incapable of fighting on its own. Its strength comes from diminutive larva living in its bloodstream. When threatened, masses of larva move into egg-shaped incubators near the top of its carapace and rapidly transform into vicious locusts, dramatically shortening their own lifespans in the process.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 14\
**Hit Points** 135 (18d10 + 36)\
**Speed** 30 ft.

**Senses** darkvision 30 ft., tremorsense 90 ft., passive Perception 15\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 5 (2,300 XP)

## Traits

**Burrow.** Standard zerg burrow.

## Actions

**Bite.** *Melee:* +7 to hit, 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (2d6+4) slashing.

**Partial Burrow.** Gains three-quarters cover while partially burrowed.

**Spawn Locust** *(must be burrowed).* Creates two [[Locust|Locusts]] in nearest unoccupied squares. Each dies after 1 minute and acts on its own initiative.

**Tunnel (Recharge 5–6)** *(must be burrowed).* Tunnels to a location within 30 ft. connected by ground. Cannot tunnel through substances harder than stone.
