# Queen

![[assets/Queen.jpg]]

*Large monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Queens are essential to any zerg colony. They tirelessly tend eggs and ensure rapid growth, but they also ensure the expansion of the swarm and protection of their brood. They heal nearby zerg, rescue them from death's door, spawn creep tumors, and spread creep: a pulsating infection that runs through the ground, nurturing the zerg it touches while healing and strengthening them.

## Traits

**Creep Tumor.** Spend 1 hour to create a [[Creep Tumor]] on a surface within 5 ft. (incapacitated while doing so). Once per week.

**Tend Eggs.** 2 hours to double an egg's growth rate for 1 day. Once per egg per day.

## Actions

**Spines.** *Ranged:* +5 to hit, 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d10+2) piercing.

**Talon.** *Melee:* +6 to hit, 10 ft. *Hit:* 21 (4d8+4) slashing.

**Transfuse (Recharge 5–6).** Target non-undead, non-construct within 30 ft. (not itself): heals 47 (6d8+20) HP.
