# Lurker

![[assets/Lurker.jpg]]

*Large monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Lurkers began as an experimental mutation of the hydralisk. They sacrifice some versatility for the ability to remain hidden while attacking. Their deadly subterranean spines allow them to disable and dismember creatures while remaining entirely hidden from view. They can tunnel to new positions when enemies converge, but their poor eyesight and slow legs make scouting parties important.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 14\
**Hit Points** 97 (13d10 + 26)\
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 3 (-4) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +6\
**Senses** darkvision 30 ft., tremorsense 90 ft., passive Perception 14\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

**Ambusher.** Advantage on attack rolls against surprised creatures.

**Burrow.** Standard 1-minute zerg burrow.

## Actions

**Claw.** *Melee:* +6 to hit, 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6+4) slashing.

**Partial Burrow.** Gains three-quarters cover instead of total cover.

**Subterranean Spines** *(must be burrowed).* 60-ft. line, 5 ft. wide. DC 14 Dex save: 14 (4d6) piercing + knocked prone on fail; half, not prone on success.

**Tunnel (Recharge 5–6)** *(must be burrowed).* Tunnels to a location within 30 ft. connected by ground, leaving a faint trail. Cannot tunnel through substances harder than stone.
