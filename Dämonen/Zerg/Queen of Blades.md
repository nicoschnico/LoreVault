# Queen of Blades

![[assets/Queen_of_Blades.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity, chaotic evil*

## Description

The Queen of Blades is no mere monarch of beasts, but the chosen executioner, general, and confidante of [[Dämonen/Prinzen/Graz'zt|Graz'zt]]. Where the Dark Prince seduces courts, corrupts rulers, and topples nations through vanity and desire, she follows as the blade behind the smile: the hand that turns corruption into conquest.

Legends claim she was once mortal, elevated through a pact sealed in blood, obsession, and absolute ambition. Graz'zt granted her a fragment of abyssal sovereignty and bound the zerg swarm to her will, transforming her into a psionic war-queen whose presence can dominate both demon and beast. She governs the swarm in her master's name, crushes rebellious cults, and leads abyssal campaigns whenever Graz'zt desires terror rather than temptation.

Among the cults of Graz'zt she is feared as the Black Consort, the Prince's right hand woman, and the herald of his final patience. Where she appears, seduction has failed and annihilation begins.

## Traits

**Abyssal Hive Regent.** Mentally connected to all zerg and willing cultists of [[Dämonen/Prinzen/Graz'zt|Graz'zt]] within 1 mile; can command, direct, and communicate with them with no action required.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If she fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

**Profane Majesty.** All damage done by the Queen of Blades is magical. Creatures charmed by her or by magic associated with [[Dämonen/Prinzen/Graz'zt|Graz'zt]] have disadvantage on saving throws against her abilities.

**Spellcasting.** 20th-level spellcaster, Intelligence (spell save DC 23, +15 to hit).
- Cantrips: *chill touch, fire bolt, mage hand, minor illusion, prestidigitation*
- 1st (4): *charm person, command, disguise self, shield, thunderwave*
- 2nd (3): *crown of madness, detect thoughts, hold person, mirror image, misty step*
- 3rd (3): *counterspell, fear, fireball, hypnotic pattern, vampiric touch*
- 4th (3): *blight, confusion, dimension door, greater invisibility*
- 5th (3): *dominate person, hold monster, mislead, telekinesis*
- 6th (2): *chain lightning, disintegrate, eyebite, mass suggestion*
- 7th (2): *forcecage, plane shift, reverse gravity, teleport*
- 8th (1): *dominate monster, feeblemind, maddening darkness*
- 9th (1): *foresight, power word kill, psychic scream*

**Abyssal Telekinesis.** Improved *telekinesis* at will, range 150 ft., Gargantuan or smaller, up to 15,000 lbs, two targets simultaneously.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Four attacks (Abyssal Lance or Spined Wings each). Can replace two with Soul Tithe, Crushing Grip, or Abyssal Telekinesis.

**Soul Tithe (1/Day).** The Queen of Blades targets any number of willing creatures, charmed creatures, or cultists of [[Dämonen/Prinzen/Graz'zt|Graz'zt]] within 30 feet. She can drain each target of any number of hit points up to its hit point maximum, regaining one quarter that many hit points. She can instead spend 30 hit points per spell level to regain expended spell slots.

**Crushing Grip.** Grappled target: DC 23 Str save or 65 (10d12) force damage (half on success).

**Abyssal Lance.** *Ranged:* +15 to hit, 200/600 ft. *Hit:* 29 (5d8+7) psychic damage plus 10 (3d6) fire damage.

**Spined Wings.** *Melee:* +15 to hit, 15 ft. *Hit:* 33 (4d12+7) piercing. DC 23 Str or knocked prone.

**Summon Zerg (1/Day).** Summons zerg creatures totaling CR 10 or less within 60 ft. Summoned creatures appear marked by abyssal sigils and act as extensions of Graz'zt's will.

## Legendary Actions (3/round)

- **Attack.** Abyssal Lance or Spined Wings.
- **Crush (2 actions).** Uses Crushing Grip.
- **Move.** Up to half speed.
- **Abyssal Telekinesis (2 actions).** Uses Abyssal Telekinesis.
