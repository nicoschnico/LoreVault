# Zergling

![[assets/Zergling.jpg]]

*Medium monstrosity (zerg), unaligned*

## Description

Zerglings hatch in sets of two or three and are the most numerous creatures in the swarm. Individually they are only slightly stronger than a drone, but their strength comes from sheer numbers. A single zergling is easy prey; a pack of thirty or more is another matter entirely.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 13\
**Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 5)\
**Speed** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 3 (-4) | 13 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5\
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 13\
**Languages** —\
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)

## Traits

**Ambusher.** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.

**Burrow.** Standard 1-minute zerg burrow.

**Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft. of the creature and isn't incapacitated.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Two Claw attacks.

**Claw.** *Melee:* +5 to hit, 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4+3) slashing.
