_Wondrous Item, uncommon, single use_

This golden acorn, no larger than a coin, is cool to the touch and hums with chaotic energy.
- As a reaction when you fail a saving throw, you can crush the acorn in your fist to reroll the save. You must take the new result.
    
- When used, nearby flora rustles as if cheering—or laughing.

| d6  | Wild Fate Effect                                                                                     |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | You turn invisible for 1 round (no concentration, ends if you attack).                               |
| 2   | You are Silenced (as the _silence_ spell, centered on you) for 1 minute.                             |
| 3   | You teleport 30 feet in a random direction (roll d8 for compass direction).                          |
| 4   | A chorus of giggling voices echoes around you, giving you advantage on Charisma checks for 1 minute. |
| 5   | A crown of butterflies forms around your head, granting resistance to psychic damage for 1 minute.   |
| 6   | You gain **1d4 temporary hit points** at the start of each of your turns for 1 minute.               |
Once the acorn is used, it crumbles into stardust and vanishes with a faint chime.
