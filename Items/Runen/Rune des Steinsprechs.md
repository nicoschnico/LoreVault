**Rune of the Stonevoice**  

You touch a stone surface or object no larger than a 5-foot cube and awaken within it a dim consciousness. For the next 10 minutes, the stone can communicate with you, sharing impressions of creatures, objects, events, weather, and disturbances that occurred nearby within the last day.

While the rune remains active, you may shape the surrounding earth and stone. Difficult terrain caused by rock or rubble can become stable and traversable, or stable ground can instead crack and shift into difficult terrain filled with fissures and unstable footing.

Until the rune fades, you can speak and understand Terran, sense creatures merged with stone nearby, and command the earth to harmlessly release them from their prison.
