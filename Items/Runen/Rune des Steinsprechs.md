**Rune of the Stonevoice**  

The rune must be inscribed on a stone surface or object measuring no more than 5 feet cubed for ten minutes to trigger the awakening of dim consciousness. For the next 10 minutes, the stone can communicate with you, sharing impressions of creatures, objects, events, weather, and disturbances that occurred nearby within the last day. 

While the rune remains active, you may shape the surrounding earth and stone (10ft cube). Difficult terrain caused by rock or rubble can become stable and traversable, or stable ground can instead crack and shift into difficult terrain filled with fissures and unstable footing.

Until the rune fades, you can speak and understand Terran, sense creatures merged with stone nearby, and command the earth to harmlessly release them from their prison.
