Pixi Dust:
**Pixie Dust** is a powerful narcotic that is harvested in the **[Feywild](https://adventurers-and-adversaries.fandom.com/wiki/Feywild "Feywild")** and smuggled around various worlds. It is used as a means to induce sleeping and meditation. However, when it is used as an illicit drug, the vivid dreaming can become harrowing nightmares. Withdrawals can result in extreme insomnia, unable to rest and recover as one would normally.
