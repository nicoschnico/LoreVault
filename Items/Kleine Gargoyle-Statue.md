* **Phantom Applause**: Once per day, the user can speak a command word to cause the statue to emit faint sounds of applause, cheers, or admiring whispers. These sounds last for 1 minute and can be heard within 30 feet.
    
- **Glimpse of Glory**: As a bonus action, the user can activate the statue to create a shimmering, illusory aura around themselves that makes them appear larger than life. This effect lasts for 1 minute and grants advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks during this time.
    
- **Unintended Echo**: The magic of the mirror feeds on self-perception. Each time Phantom Applause or Glimpse of Glory is used, there’s a small, unintended consequence:
    
    - Roll 1d6 after using the ability:
        - 1–3: A faint but audible snicker, jeer, or sarcastic clap is heard, betraying the illusion to anyone nearby.
        - 4–5: The user’s reflection briefly flickers, showing a distorted or exaggerated image of their true self—oversized features, flaws, or insecurities. This effect lasts a moment but may be noticed by onlookers.
        - 6: The illusion works perfectly without any flaw, leaving the user confident in their magnificence.
