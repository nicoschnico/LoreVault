# Giant Parasect (Large)

*Large monstrosity, unaligned*

Eine kleinere, aber weiterhin hochgefährliche Parasect-Variante. Sie nutzt dieselbe Grapple-Taktik, kombiniert mit lähmenden Sporenwolken.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 68 (8d10 + 24)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 11 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 5 (-3) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +2  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned  
**Senses** blindsight 20 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

## Traits

**Amphibious.** The Giant Parasect can breathe air and water.

**Sure-Footed.** The Giant Parasect has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws against effects that would knock it prone.

**Fungal Symbiosis.** The enormous mushroom growing from the creature's shell has completely fused with its nervous system.

**Spore-Laden Shell.** Whenever the Giant Parasect takes 10 or more damage from a single attack, spores erupt from its mushroom. Creatures within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of their next turn.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The Giant Parasect makes two Claw attacks.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 14) if it is Medium or smaller.

The Giant Parasect has two claws, each of which can grapple one creature.

**Crushing Claw.** One creature grappled by the Giant Parasect takes 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. The Giant Parasect can then move the creature up to 5 feet without ending the grapple.

**Paralytic Spore Cloud (Recharge 5–6).** The mushroom releases a dense cloud of yellow spores in a 15-foot radius around the Giant Parasect.

Each other creature in the area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 10 (3d6) poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned this way, its speed is halved.

If it fails the save by 5 or more, it is also paralyzed until the end of its next turn.

The creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the poisoned effect on a success.

- Total height: roughly 12–15 ft. / 3.5–4.5 m
