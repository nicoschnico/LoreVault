# Giant Parasect (Huge)

*Huge monstrosity, unaligned*

Ein elefantengroßer, krabbenartiger Jäger mit baumhohem Pilzaufsatz. Er bindet Beute mit Scheren und macht Nahkampf durch Sporeneruptionen gefährlich.

## Stats

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 105 (10d12 + 40)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 21 (+5) | 10 (+0) | 19 (+4) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 5 (-3) |

**Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7  
**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +3  
**Damage Resistances** poison  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned  
**Senses** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

## Traits

**Amphibious.** The Giant Parasect can breathe air and water.

**Sure-Footed.** The Giant Parasect has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock it prone.

**Fungal Symbiosis.** The enormous mushroom growing from the creature's shell has completely fused with its nervous system.

**Spore-Laden Shell.** Whenever the Giant Parasect takes 15 or more damage from a single attack, spores erupt from its mushroom. Creatures within 10 feet of it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of their next turn.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The Giant Parasect makes two Claw attacks.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 16) if it is Large or smaller.

The Giant Parasect has two claws, each of which can grapple one creature.

**Crushing Claw.** A creature already grappled by the Giant Parasect takes 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. The Giant Parasect can then move the creature up to 10 feet without ending the grapple.

**Paralytic Spore Cloud (Recharge 5–6).** The enormous mushroom violently contracts and releases a dense yellow cloud of spores in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on the Giant Parasect.

Each other creature in the area must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 14 (4d6) poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned this way, its speed is halved.

If the creature fails the saving throw by 5 or more, it is also paralyzed until the end of its next turn.

A poisoned creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
